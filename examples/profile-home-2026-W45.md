## Reasoning

The Normal template from the profile. Thursday's Pilates class is fixed. Monday and Friday are Strength Sessions after a Mobility Session (`mobility, strength`). There's no routine of their own in `athlete/bank/`, so every Mobility Session is composed from the bodyweight mobility Exercises: hips, thoracic spine, ankles, hamstrings and shoulders, 2 rounds at every Level. Tuesday's stands alone on a Recovery Day and uses the long holds Monday's didn't. The strength is composed from the bodyweight and dumbbell Exercises: each Session covers squat, hinge, push, pull, carry and core, and Friday uses none of Monday's strength Exercises. A Normal week gives 3 rounds. Saturday swaps mobility for the yin yoga video.
