# Athletes can add Personal Workouts and Exercises, checked against the Bank's principles

The Workout Bank is the Core Bank (curated, in this repo) plus the athlete's own Personal Workouts and Personal Exercises, kept in their athlete repo. The Core Bank can never cover everything an athlete does (a physio routine, a machine at their gym), so it stays a baseline of foundational, widely applicable options, and the rest is added per athlete. Previously a Bank gap could never be filled with an invented Workout; a Personal one may now fill it, but only after it passes a written checklist of the Bank's principles (applied by Claude when it's added) and the mechanical frontmatter checks in `icu.py`. The alternatives were a curated-only Bank, which leaves real athletes with permanent gaps, and letting athletes fork and edit `bank/`, which loses the shared baseline and any check.

## Consequences

- The maintainer's PT routine moves out of the Core Bank and becomes a Personal Workout that fills a Mobility Session.
- Adding one goes through a dedicated skill, which Setup offers when it finds a gap.
