# Runs, PT and walks are reported at the Check-in, not synced

Runs (Apple Watch → Strava), PT and walks stay in Strava only. They are mentioned verbally at the Check-in and noted in the Week Record. intervals.icu's API won't return Strava-sourced activities because of Strava's API terms. The fixes considered were HealthFit or Intervals Companion syncing Apple Health directly, or Claude logging manual activities, and all were rejected to avoid another app and duplicate records. As a result, intervals.icu's fitness/fatigue/form numbers reflect **rides only**, and Load Warnings combine those numbers with Week Level history and judgement about reported runs. The history also starts from zero (no Strava backfill), so the number-based warning rules are unreliable for roughly the first six weeks.
