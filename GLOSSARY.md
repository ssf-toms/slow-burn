# Slow Burn

A personal system for planning a week of training ahead of time: indoor/outdoor cycling, running, kettlebell/band strength and physical therapy, aimed at aerobic fitness and durability rather than competition.

## Language

### Workouts

**Workout**:
A reusable definition of a training activity (structure, targets, instructions) kept in the Workout Bank. A Session is a Workout placed on a day.
_Avoid_: Session (when meaning the definition), template, routine

**Workout Bank**:
The small curated set of Workouts that every Week Plan draws from.
_Avoid_: Library, catalogue, database

**Ladder**:
An ordered sequence of Workouts of one Quality type, each slightly harder than the last. You move up a step after completing the current one at an effort of 7/10 or below.
_Avoid_: Progression, plan, series

### Days and sessions

**Session**:
The main planned activity for a day. A day has exactly one Session, optionally followed by a Strength Add-on.
_Avoid_: Workout (when meaning the scheduled thing), activity, training

**Quality Session**:
A ride or run with deliberate time above endurance intensity (tempo, sweet spot, threshold, VO2max).
_Avoid_: Hard workout, intervals day, key session

**Endurance Session**:
A ride or run held at easy, Zone 2 intensity.
_Avoid_: Base ride, easy ride, Z2

**Strength Session**:
A kettlebell and/or resistance-band session that stands as a day's Session, usually combined with a PT Session on a Recovery Day.
_Avoid_: Gym, lifting, conditioning

**Strength Add-on**:
A short (~15 min) kettlebell/band block done straight after an Endurance Session or a Test Session's easy spin; it never follows a Quality Session.
_Avoid_: Finisher, extra

**Test Session**:
A day built around the FTP test, followed by an easy spin.
_Avoid_: Test day, benchmark

**PT Session**:
The ~30 min physical-therapy routine, done for maintenance.
_Avoid_: Mobility, rehab, stretching

**Recovery Day**:
A day whose Session is a PT Session, a Strength Session (alone or combined with PT), or a walk. Never on the bike. One to three per week, depending on the Week Level.
_Avoid_: Rest day, off day

### Planning

**Week Plan**:
The seven Sessions chosen for one upcoming week, decided before the week starts.
_Avoid_: Schedule, program, block

**Week Template**:
The fixed Mon–Sun pattern of Session kinds that every Week Plan starts from, adjusted by the Week Level.
_Avoid_: Default week, pattern

**Week Level**:
How hard a Week Plan is meant to be, chosen at the Check-in. Levels scale mainly by how many cardio days the week has, not by session length: **Light** (0–1 Quality Sessions, 3 Recovery Days), **Normal** (2 Quality Sessions, 2 Recovery Days), **Push** (2 Quality Sessions, 1 Recovery Day).
_Avoid_: Intensity, difficulty, load

**Check-in**:
The weekly conversation in which last week is reviewed and the next Week Plan, with its Week Level, is agreed.
_Avoid_: Planning session, review

**Adjustment**:
A mid-week reshuffle of the remaining Sessions of the current Week Plan when life intervenes.
_Avoid_: Reschedule, replan

**Week Record**:
The lasting account of one week: its Week Plan, the Week Level and the reasoning behind it, Adjustments, and what was actually done and reported.
_Avoid_: Log, journal, history

**Load Warning**:
An advisory raised at a Check-in suggesting a Light week. It is raised after recent hard weeks or when ride-based fitness/fatigue numbers look stretched. It never overrides your choice.
_Avoid_: Alert, block, overtraining flag
