---
id: wall-slide
name: "Wall slide"
pattern: mobility
equipment: [bodyweight]
reps: "10"
---

Back, head and forearms on a wall, elbows at 90°. Slide your arms up as far as they go without your back arching or your forearms leaving the wall, 3 s up, 3 s down.

Progress: step the feet in closer to the wall.
