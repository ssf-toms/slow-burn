---
id: worlds-greatest-stretch
name: "World's greatest stretch"
pattern: mobility
equipment: [bodyweight]
reps: "5/side"
---

From a long lunge, hand inside the front foot, drop the back knee, then reach the other arm to the ceiling and follow it with your eyes. Slow, 2 s at the top.

Progress: back knee off the floor, then a 5 s hold at the top.
