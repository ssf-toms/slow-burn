---
id: couch-stretch
name: "Couch stretch"
pattern: mobility
equipment: [bodyweight]
hold: "45 s/side"
---

Kneel with your back shin up a wall or sofa, other foot forward. Squeeze the glute of the back leg and stay tall until you feel the front of that hip.

Progress: move the knee closer to the wall, then raise the arm on the stretched side.
