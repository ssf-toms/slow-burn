---
id: hamstring-fold
name: "Half-kneeling hamstring stretch"
pattern: mobility
equipment: [bodyweight]
hold: "45 s/side"
---

Half-kneeling, slide the front heel forward with the toes up and the knee nearly straight. Hinge forward from the hips with a flat back until you feel the hamstring.

Progress: straighten the front knee fully, then reach for the toes.
