---
id: ankle-knee-to-wall
name: "Knee-to-wall ankle rock"
pattern: mobility
equipment: [bodyweight]
reps: "10/side"
---

Half-kneeling facing a wall, front toes a hand's width from it. Rock the knee forward over the toes to touch the wall, heel stays down.

Progress: move the foot back a little at a time, then hold the last rep for 10 s.
