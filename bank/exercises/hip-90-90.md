---
id: hip-90-90
name: "90/90 hip switch"
pattern: mobility
equipment: [bodyweight]
reps: "6/side"
---

Sit with both knees bent at 90°, one leg in front and one to the side. Rotate both knees over to the other side slowly, chest tall, hands behind you if you need them.

Progress: hands off the floor, then lean over the front shin for 3 s on each side.
