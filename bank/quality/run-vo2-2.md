# Run VO2max 2: 5×3 min

- **Warm-up:** 15 min easy ({easy_run_pace}), with 4 × 20 s strides at the end.
- **Main:** 5 × 3 min at VO2max pace ({threshold_run_pace} −10–15 s/km), RPE 8–9/10, with 3 min easy jog between.
- **Cool-down:** 10 min easy.

Very hard. The last minute of each rep should be uncomfortable but repeatable. If you can't hold the pace, finish the reps by effort and repeat this step next time. On hills, in wind or on a bad day, run by RPE and let the pace go.
