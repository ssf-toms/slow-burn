# Run Threshold 2: 3×8 min

- **Warm-up:** 15 min easy ({easy_run_pace}), with 4 × 20 s strides at the end.
- **Main:** 3 × 8 min at threshold pace ({threshold_run_pace}, ±5 s/km), RPE 7–8/10, with 2 min easy jog between.
- **Cool-down:** 10 min easy.

Hard but controlled: the pace you could race for about an hour. Breathing is deep and rhythmic. If the last rep slows by more than 10 s/km, repeat this step next time. On hills, in wind or on a bad day, run by RPE and let the pace go.
