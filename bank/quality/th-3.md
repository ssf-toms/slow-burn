# Threshold 3: 3×10 min @ 97%

```intervals
Warmup
- 4m 50%
- 4m 60%
- 2m 70%
- 1m 105%
- 4m 55%

3x
- 10m 97%
- 5m 50%

Cooldown
- 6m 50%
```

Hard but controlled: you could hold it for about an hour if you had to. Breathing is deep and rhythmic. The 1 min opener in the warmup is meant to feel brisk, not hard.
