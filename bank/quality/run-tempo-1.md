# Run Tempo 1: 3×6 min

- **Warm-up:** 15 min easy ({easy_run_pace}), with 4 × 20 s strides at the end.
- **Main:** 3 × 6 min at tempo pace ({threshold_run_pace} +15–20 s/km), RPE 6/10, with 2 min easy jog between.
- **Cool-down:** 10 min easy.

Comfortably hard: steady breathing, a few words at a time. Start each block at the slow end of the range. On hills, in wind or on a bad day, run by RPE and let the pace go.
