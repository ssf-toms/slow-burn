# Steady swim 2000 m

Steady (RPE 5/10): working, but you could say a sentence at the wall.

- **Warm-up:** 300 m easy, any stroke.
- **Drills:** 4×50 m, 15 s rest: catch-up, then fingertip drag, twice through.
- **Main set:** 3×400 m steady freestyle, 30 s rest. Even pace: the last 100 m of each about as quick as the first.
- **Cool-down:** 300 m easy, mixing in backstroke.

Alternative: swap this for [end-ride-60](../endurance/end-ride-60.md) or [run-easy-6-8](../endurance/run-easy-6-8.md) on the day.
