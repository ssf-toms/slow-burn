# Drill swim 1800 m

Easy to steady (RPE 4/10), with the focus on form rather than pace.

- **Warm-up:** 300 m easy, any stroke.
- **Drills:** 8×50 m, 15 s rest. Odd lengths drill (catch-up, fingertip drag, single arm, 6-kick switch), even lengths swim.
- **Main set:** 4×200 m easy to steady freestyle, 30 s rest. Hold the form from the drills as you tire.
- **Cool-down:** 300 m easy, mixing in backstroke.

Alternative: swap this for [end-ride-50](../endurance/end-ride-50.md) or [run-easy-6-8](../endurance/run-easy-6-8.md) on the day.
