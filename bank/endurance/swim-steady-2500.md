# Steady swim 2500 m

Steady (RPE 5/10), the longest swim. Back off to easy if your stroke falls apart.

- **Warm-up:** 400 m easy, any stroke.
- **Drills:** 4×50 m, 15 s rest: catch-up, then fingertip drag, twice through.
- **Main set:** 2×800 m steady freestyle, 45 s rest. Settle in for the first 200 m of each.
- **Cool-down:** 300 m easy, mixing in backstroke.

Alternative: swap this for [end-ride-60](../endurance/end-ride-60.md) or [run-easy-8-10](../endurance/run-easy-8-10.md) on the day.
