# Easy swim 1500 m

All easy (RPE 3–4/10): you could chat at the wall after every rep.

- **Warm-up:** 300 m easy, any stroke.
- **Drills:** 4×50 m, 15 s rest: catch-up, then fingertip drag, twice through.
- **Main set:** 8×100 m easy freestyle, 20 s rest. Long, relaxed strokes; breathe every 3 if it's comfortable.
- **Cool-down:** 200 m easy, mixing in backstroke.

Alternative: swap this for [run-easy-5-6](../endurance/run-easy-5-6.md) or [end-ride-50](../endurance/end-ride-50.md) on the day.
