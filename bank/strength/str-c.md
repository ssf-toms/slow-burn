# Strength C: carry and core

Warm-up (2 min): 10 kettlebell halos each way, 10 band face pulls, 10 bodyweight squats.

4 rounds (rest 60–90 s between rounds):
- Kettlebell suitcase carry × 40 s/side (tall posture)
- Kettlebell clean and press × 6/side
- Step-up holding the kettlebell × 10/side
- Band face pull × 15
- Band Pallof press × 10/side

Core finisher (2 rounds, ~5 min, minimal rest):
- Side plank with reach-through × 6/side
- Bird dog × 8/side (3 s hold)
- Kettlebell Russian twist × 10/side (slow, heels down if needed)

Progress: longer carries (up to 60 s), more press reps.
