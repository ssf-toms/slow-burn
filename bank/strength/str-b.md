# Strength B: single leg

Warm-up (2 min): 10 lunges (alternating), 10 glute bridges, 10 band pull-aparts.

4 rounds (rest 60–90 s between rounds):
- Split squat, kettlebell goblet-held × 8/side
- Single-leg RDL, kettlebell in opposite hand × 8/side
- Kettlebell floor press × 10/side
- Band pull-apart × 15
- Side plank × 30 s/side

Core finisher (2 rounds, ~5 min, minimal rest):
- Bear hold (knees 2 cm off the floor) × 30 s
- Reverse crunch × 10 (slow)
- Slow bicycle crunch × 10/side

Progress: add reps (up to 12/side), or pause 1 s at the bottom.
