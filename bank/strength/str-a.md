# Strength A: hinge and push

Warm-up (2 min): 10 bodyweight squats, 10 hip hinges, 10 arm circles.

4 rounds (rest 60–90 s between rounds):
- Kettlebell swing × 15
- Goblet squat × 10 (3 s down)
- Band row × 12
- Push-up × 8–12 (knees or incline is fine)
- Dead bug × 8/side

Core finisher (2 rounds, ~5 min, minimal rest):
- Plank shoulder taps × 10/side (hips still)
- Hollow hold × 20–30 s
- Glute bridge march × 10/side

Progress: add 2–3 reps per move until rounds feel easy, then slow the tempo.
