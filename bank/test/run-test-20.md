# Run test: 20 min time trial

- **Warm-up:** 15 min easy ({easy_run_pace}), with 4 × 20 s strides, then 2 min easy.
- **Main:** 20 min as hard as you can hold evenly, on a flat route or a track. Start at about your current threshold pace ({threshold_run_pace}) and only push on in the last 5 min.
- **Cool-down:** 10 min easy.

Your average pace for the 20 min is your new threshold pace. Read it from your watch, then update `threshold_run_pace` in your Athlete Profile and tell me at the Check-in. Pick a calm, dry day: wind, hills or heat make the result too slow.
